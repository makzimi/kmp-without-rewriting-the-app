What changed in the v3 architecture

Three things moved off the critical path so the order pipeline could survive a region failure.

  • Order events now publish through a kafka topic instead of a synchronous RPC.
  • Inventory reservations use a CRDT backed by Riak.
  • Payment authorization is idempotent and retried by the gateway, not the client.
  • Read paths are served from a regional cache with a 50ms staleness budget.

See ADR-0042 for the full migration plan.
